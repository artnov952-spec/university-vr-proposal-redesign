Cloudplus UI Pack supplied by the owner: cloudplus-ui-pack.zip.
The original erp-kit-clean/css/kit.css is included unchanged in ui-kit/.
Buttons, semantic tokens, radii, focus and transition timings are used by
the proposal. Project colors are overridden in proposal-theme.css.
The generic kit.js demo event handlers are intentionally not loaded:
the proposal and working stand have their own stateful behavior.

SVG icon sprite and individual icons are from the supplied pack.

Upstream: https://github.com/vercel/geist-font
Golos Text fonts and their OFL notices are retained in assets/fonts/.

Used from the client's official website for this demonstration proposal.
It is not a new brand mark or a claim of official endorsement.

Lighthouse was studied as an interaction/storytelling reference only.
Its photographs, copy and brand artwork are not included.

E-reyting visual kit supplied by the owner on 2026-10-26: e-reyting-kit.zip.
Its burgundy palette, hairline data tables, active navigation rail, compact
status patterns, 140ms timing and numeric typography are adapted in
proposal-theme.css and e-reyting-theme.css. Inter and DejaVu Sans Mono font
files are included from that pack in e-reyting-fonts/. The original kit
is retained outside deployment. Its static example people, departments,
points, deadlines and approval statements are not copied into this demo.
Rounded corners intentionally remain softer than the kit's 4/8px baseline
to preserve the owner's earlier explicit request. No generic demo script
from either supplied kit is executed.
