Integrated educational CG scene frames, 2026-10-27.

20 fictional scenarios, five whole-frame checkpoint renders per scenario.
Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool from the project's own original
CG environments. No paid external API, photographic cutouts, sprite atlases,
independent person/prop scaling or third-party map imagery is used here.

Art direction: fixed wide 2:1 camera, preserve architecture and perspective,
natural human/vehicle sizes, grounded contacts, consistent light and shadows.
Native images: 1774 x 887 pixels. Web copies: JPEG quality 95, no upscaling.
Original native PNG outputs are retained in the owner's local generation files.
The road base has a straight crossing perpendicular to the roadway.

Content and people are fictional educational concepts for an Uzbekistan
university proposal, not official procedures, incident records or real people.
Checkpoint states may vary in pose and details. These are not a continuous
3D simulation, measured reconstruction, headset telemetry or a training rubric
approved by the university. Final methodology requires university approval.
